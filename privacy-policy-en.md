# Privacy Policy — ניקוד לילדים (The Letter Journey)

**Last updated: 28 July 2026**

ניקוד לילדים ("the app") is an educational app that helps young children learn the
Hebrew alphabet and vowel signs (niqqud). It is developed and published by Guy Ben
Moshe ("we", "us").

This app is designed for children. We have built it so that it collects **no personal
information whatsoever** — not from children, and not from adults.

This policy covers the app on **both Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS)**.
The app behaves identically on both.

## The short version

- We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking.
- We do **not** require an account, a login, or an email address to use the app.
- The app contains **no ads** and **no links to social networks**.
- We do not operate a server. Apart from making the optional purchase, which the app
  store handles, the app works entirely offline.

## What the app stores on your device

The app saves two small preferences **on your device only** — using Android's own
local storage (Jetpack DataStore) on Android, and the standard preferences system on
iOS. This information never leaves your device, and we never see it:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your chosen interface language (Hebrew, English, or Russian) | So the app opens in the same language next time. |
| Whether the one-time "unlock everything" purchase has been made | So paid content stays unlocked without asking the store on every launch. |

Uninstalling the app removes both.

## Permissions the app requests

On Android the app declares only the following permissions, and none of them is used to
identify you or collect information about you:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `VIBRATE` | Gentle haptic feedback when a child taps a letter or solves the door game. |
| `com.android.vending.BILLING` | To offer the single optional in-app purchase through Google Play. |
| `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `WAKE_LOCK` | Merged in by the Google Play Billing library. The app itself makes no network requests of its own and sends nothing anywhere. |
| `com.gbt.nikudkids.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION` | An automatically generated, app-private permission that keeps the Billing library's internal broadcast receiver from being reachable by other apps. |

The app requests **no** access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos,
files, or the advertising ID.

## In-app purchase

The app offers a single, optional, one-time purchase that unlocks the full content.
There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

On Android, purchases are handled **entirely by Google Play**. On iOS they are handled
entirely by Apple. We never see, receive, or store your name, payment card, billing
address, or Google/Apple account details. That transaction is governed by Google's (or
Apple's) own privacy policy, not ours.

In line with Google Play's Families policy and Apple's rules for children's apps, the
purchase screen sits behind a **parental gate**: an adult must read a number written out
in words and type it in before any payment can begin. A child cannot reach the purchase
screen unaided.

## Children's privacy

This app is intended for young children. It collects no data at all, so there is no
personal information of a child for us to use, share, sell, or disclose — to anyone, for
any purpose. We comply with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the
EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's Families policy.

The app contains no behavioural advertising, no contextual advertising, no analytics SDKs,
and no third-party code that could collect information.

The only way to leave the app is an optional contact-email link on the About screen, which
sits behind a parental gate so that a child cannot open it unaided.

## Data we receive from the app stores

If you choose to contact us by email, we will see your email address and whatever you write
to us — simply because you sent it. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not add it to
any mailing list.

Google Play and Apple may provide us with aggregate, anonymous statistics about downloads,
crashes and ratings. This information is not linked to any individual and cannot identify
you or your child.

## Data deletion

Because we hold no account and collect no data, there is nothing for us to delete on a
server. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device. If you have
emailed us and would like that correspondence deleted, write to the address below and we
will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the
"last updated" date above will change. Because the app collects no data, we do not expect
material changes.
